Focus: funded research and innovation projects (CORDIS, EU Horizon, national programmes) and the organisations taking part, as signals of a startup's technology and partners.

- One record per project, `kind` project. `title` is "ACRONYM: full title" when both are shown.
- `website`: the project's page.
- Fields: programme, project_id, coordinator, participants (companies first; mark SMEs when stated), countries, eu_contribution, total_cost, start, end, status, objective (one sentence), trl, results.
- A startup or SME taking part that the documents describe in their own right may also get its own record, `kind` startup, with the fields country and what_it_does.
- `quote`: the line with the objective, or the one naming the coordinator and the contribution.
- Gaps: projects without participants or amounts on this page, and SMEs named without a website.
