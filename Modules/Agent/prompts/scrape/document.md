Focus: one document such as an annual report, pitch deck, financial statement, market study or research paper: its figures and statements that matter to an investor.

- One record per figure or statement, `kind` figure (numbers: revenue, result, headcount, market size, unit costs, ...) or the kind of the thing it describes (a startup, a funding round, a project). `title` names the figure with its subject and period (e.g. "Acme Ocean net revenue 2025").
- Fields: subject, metric, value, unit, period, comparison (the previous period or target, when stated), basis (audited, estimate, forecast, as stated).
- Keep forecasts and targets apart from reported figures, and say which is which in `details`.
- `quote`: the sentence or table line with the number, copied exactly.
- Gaps: figures an investor would expect in this kind of document that are missing (e.g. no cash position in a financial statement), and statements without a source or a date.
