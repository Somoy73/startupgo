Focus: startups and companies listed or described in the documents (directories, portfolios, pipelines, accelerator cohorts, investor lists).

- One record per company, `kind` startup (or company for an established firm). `title` is the company's name only, without a legal suffix unless it is part of the brand.
- Some directories (investment pipelines, matchmaking platforms) list profiles under a project or product title and show the company's name only on the profile's own page. Then make one record per profile anyway: `title` is the profile's title as shown, the field company is left out, and one gap says the company names are on the profile pages (the agent then opens each profile page for the company and its website).
- `website`: the company's own site when the page links or names it.
- Fields: country, city, sector, what_it_does (one sentence), stage (as stated: pre-seed, seed, series A, revenue, ...), founded, team_size, funding_raised, investors, customers_or_pilots, ocean_impact (what ocean problem it addresses, as stated), looking_for (funding sought, partners, pilots, if stated).
- `quote`: the line that names the company together with what it does.
- Gaps: companies listed without a website or a description, and fields an investor needs first that are missing for most companies (stage, funding, country).
