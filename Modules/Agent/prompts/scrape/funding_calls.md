Focus: funding a startup can apply for: grants, calls for proposals, tenders, accelerator and incubator programmes, prizes, loans and equity instruments from public bodies.

- One record per call or instrument, `kind` funding_call. `title` is its official name, with the call or topic ID when shown (e.g. "HORIZON-CL6-2026-01-BIODIV-03: Restoring marine habitats").
- `website`: the call's own page when linked.
- Fields: funder, programme, call_id, status (open, forthcoming, closed, as stated), opens, deadline, budget_total, amount_per_project, funding_rate, who_can_apply, countries, topics, trl_expected, how_to_apply.
- `quote`: the line with the deadline or the budget when there is one, else the line that says what is funded.
- Gaps: calls without a deadline or a budget on this page (say to open the call page), and eligibility that is not clear (company size, country, consortium needed).
